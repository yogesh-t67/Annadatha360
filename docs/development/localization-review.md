## How to review crop names

For each row in `seed_crops.py`, confirm the `te` value is the name an AP/TG farmer would actually use (not a
textbook or Sanskritised term). Note regional variants if AP and Telangana differ.

## Not localised by design

- Market data from Agmarknet (commodity, variety, market and district names) — source is English only.
  See `docs/api/market-prices.md`.
- Codes (weather conditions, alert types, growth stages, diagnosis categories) — the app translates these.
